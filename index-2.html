Our Expertise in:
Aluminium Iron (III) Sulphate
(Ferric Alum)

As pioneers in field, we take immense pride in our expertise. Our advanced production processes ensure the creation of Ferric Alum with unparalleled purity and consistency,making it an indispensable ingredient across various sectors.

Water Treatment

Ferric Alum plays a pivotal role in purifying water, making it safe for consumption and industrial use.

Industrial Manufacturing

Ferric Alum enhances processes and products in a multitude of industries.

Thermal Power Plants

Coagulant in water treatment processes for boiler feedwater, aiding in the removal of suspended particles and impurities.

Sewage Treatment

Coagulant and flocculant for the precipitation of solids and organic matter in sewage, leading to clearer effluent.

Waste Water Treatment

Coagulation of pollutants and heavy metals in industrial effluents, contributing to water quality improvement

Refineries

Wastewater treatment to remove contaminants and pollutants, ensuring safe disposal or reuse of water resources.

Fertilizer Industries

Used in purification processes during fertiliser production to ensure product quality and prevent environmental contamination.

Steel Plants

Wastewater treatment for clarification and purification of effluents generated during manufacturing processes.

Chemical Industries

Ferric Alum is used as an Effluent treatment to remove organic and inorganic contaminants, ensuring compliance with environmental regulations.

Industrial Manufacturing

From paper to textiles, construction to ceramics, our Ferric Alum enhances processes and products in a multitude of industries. Its versatility and reliability have made it an essential component worldwide.
