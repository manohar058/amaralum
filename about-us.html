About Us

Who We Are?

Welcome to Amar Alum & Allied Chemicals

Amar Alum is the largest manufacturer of aluminium sulphate (ferric alum) in India - touching the lives of a billion souls each day

Our motto, “Purifying Water, Empowering Lives” aptly captures the essence of what we do at Amar Alum. In just 3 decades, we have become synonymous with the best quality coagulant in India.
We’re committed to ensure quality water reaches each human on the planet & every process plant be it Thermal Power Station or a Refinery - gets most clarified water reducing load at DM Plants.

Legacy

In 1991, Amar Alum’s founder Shri Nand Lal Garg, had a revolutionary idea that involved a better process for manufacturing Aluminium Iron(III) Sulphate thus, forever changing the aluminium-based water treatment sphere. Today, a billion lives derive benefit each day through potable water, electricity and oil or Kraft paper.
His dedication towards quality potable water has inspired us to ensure our product reaches the length & breadth of our nation. Since then, Amar Alum has risen to become the foremost manufacturer of Aluminium Sulphate (Ferric Alum) - a dual coagulant of Aluminium Sulphate & Ferric Sulphate in India.
Having substantial annual production capacity exceeding 70,000 MT and 3 decades of experience, our company enjoys an unblemished reputation in the market - ensuring reliable supply of raw materials, even in the face of challenging circumstances, making us a dependable partner throughout the year.

Our Vision

At Amar Alum, our vision is to be the preferred choice for Ferric Alum solutions, known for our commitments to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. We aim to contribute positively to various industries by providing essential raw materials that enhance processes and products.

Our Mission

Our mission is to produce Ferric Alum of the highest purity and consistency, utilizing advanced manufacturing processes that adhere to stringent quality standards. We are dedicated to minimising environmental impact and maximizing the value we bring to our clients, partner, employees, and the communities we operate in.
